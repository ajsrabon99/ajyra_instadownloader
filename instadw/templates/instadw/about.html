About AJYRA InstaDownloader

AJYRA InstaDownloader is a simple, fast, and user-friendly tool designed to download Instagram videos and reels effortlessly. It uses modern techniques and yt-dlp to ensure the best quality downloads, all while keeping your experience smooth.

Features

  • Download Instagram videos and reels directly.
  • Play videos in your browser before downloading.
  • High-quality video download using yt-dlp.
  • Fast and responsive user interface.

How it Works

1

Copy the Instagram video or reel URL from your browser or Instagram app.

2

Paste the URL into the input box on the home page and click "Fetch Video".

3

Once the video is ready, you can either play it in your browser or download it directly.
